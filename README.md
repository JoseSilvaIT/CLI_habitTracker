# Context

The new wave of productivity and habit tracking is something the market has successfully capitalized on. Productivity and the need to organize different aspects of our daily lives have become an obsession of our time. Various applications have emerged attempting to recreate this organized environment through tools such as Pomodoro timers, calendars, notes, and more.

However, this can sometimes make it overwhelming for users to simply schedule and manage their tasks efficiently. That is why I decided to create a free tool using JavaScript, focused on minimalism, with a minimalist design inspired by the terminals of Unix-like systems.

# Contexto

La nueva ola de la productividad y el habit tracking es algo que el mercado ha sabido explotar. Por eso, he decidido crear una herramienta gratuita a través de JavaScript, enfocada en el minimalismo, con un diseño minimalista inspirado en las terminales de los sistemas Unix-like.
